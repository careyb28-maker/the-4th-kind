Exhibit — 002 / Origin & Mission

The Message

01 / 06 Roots to reach

From Chicago Outward

The 4th Kind is an independent Hip-Hop recording collective & label based in Chicago, Illinois. Though the seeds were originally planted in Oak Park, Illinois, the group’s message, presence and impact reaches to both coasts of the US and transcends the nation as they grow worldwide.

02 / 06 DIY signal

Built By Hand

Founded by 16RAHEEM in 2016–17, the group consisted of key members Wodiene & Spacey. Starting out with SoundCloud mixtapes, DIY Music Videos & Cover arts — the group has always carried a passion to express authenticity with a competitive flame driving them to reach the peak of high quality Hip-Hop music.

03 / 06 Pressure into poetry

Basement Gems

Inspired heavily by the sound and poetry of their city, 16, Wodiene & Jsteezzy released countless tracks from 2021–2022. Each track feeling like a hidden gem, carefully crafted by young enthusiastic poets in the basement, with a deep desire to stamp themselves as generational talents in rap.

04 / 06 Momentum shared

The Wave Joins

Since 2023, the collective includes longtime associate Freewave Ant, who has made his own wave while fitting into the frame of The 4th Kind seamlessly. His ascension was an actualization of a seed planted back in days freestyling with the group in school — and he and 16 dropped their collaborative album ‘2144’ in 2024.

05 / 06 Another encounter

Outer Space Domain

‘The 4th Kind’ takes inspiration from the 70’s film Close Encounters of The Third Kind and the duo The Third Kind formed by Capital Steez. The Chicago collective catapults their sonic realm into an outer space domain — spacey synths, jazzy samples & psychedelic ambience as the conveyance for a gritty grassroots Hip-Hop movement.

06 / 06 Feeling into form

Cathartic Alchemy

With deep reflective themes of love, paranoia, hustle, dreams, pain, sacrifice, faith & betrayal — the introspection & raw vulnerability is what sets this group apart. The group understands one central idea: art is meaningless if it does not manifest as cathartic alchemy for the artist, or if it does not provoke the thought or tug at the heart strings of the listener.
